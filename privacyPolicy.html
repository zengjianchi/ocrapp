zengjianchi ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Fast Image Text Scanner collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information, and how you can contact us.

Scope This policy applies to Fast Image Text Scanner on iPhone, iPad, Android devices, and related support pages for this product.
Core Principle Image recognition is performed on-device by default. We do not upload the images you process or the recognized text to our own servers unless a feature clearly says so and you choose to use it.

1. Information We May Process

  • Information you provide directly, such as your email address and the contents of support requests, feedback, or appeals.
  • Images you choose to import, capture, or process, as well as the text recognized from those images.
  • History items, preferences, and simple usage state that you choose to keep in the app.
  • Device and diagnostic information, such as device model, operating system version, basic performance data, and crash-related signals generated by the system or SDKs when necessary.
  • Advertising-related information. If ads are enabled in the app, advertising SDKs may process device identifiers, ad interaction events, approximate location data, or other signals used to deliver and measure ads.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core features such as image import, camera capture, batch recognition, result viewing, text copying, and system sharing.
  • To store history, imported image copies, and preference settings on your device so you can continue where you left off.
  • To maintain app stability, troubleshoot issues, and improve the user experience.
  • To respond to support questions, operational requests, and compliance matters.
  • To handle security, abuse prevention, and legal obligations where permitted or required by law.

3. Local Processing and Uploads

For image recognition features, Fast Image Text Scanner processes selected images on-device by default. Recognition results, history items, and related preferences are typically stored locally on your device for later viewing or editing.

We do not upload the images you process, the recognized text, or your local history to our own servers unless a feature explicitly says it requires an upload and you intentionally trigger that action.

If you use the system share sheet to send results to another app, email service, or cloud provider, that onward processing is governed by the privacy policy of the service you choose.

4. Permissions

  • Camera permission: used when you choose to capture an image for processing.
  • Photos or media library permission: used when you choose to import an image from your device.

We do not continuously access your camera or photo library without your action. You can disable these permissions at any time in system settings, although doing so may limit related features.

If the app displays banner ads, rewarded ads, or other advertising content, the ad provider may process device identifiers, ad requests, impression and click events, and related signals used for ad delivery and measurement under its own privacy practices.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

If you keep recognition history, batch queues, preferences, or imported image copies in the app, that data is typically stored locally on your device until you delete the content, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

If you contact us through email or another support channel, we may retain the related correspondence for a reasonable period to handle follow-up issues, comply with legal obligations, and support necessary security or audit needs.

7. Data Sharing

We do not voluntarily disclose the images or recognized text you process in the app except in the following situations:

  • You ask us to do so through a sharing, export, or similar action.
  • Third-party providers process limited technical data as necessary to support ads, distribution, diagnostics, or other essential services.
  • Disclosure is required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, us, or others from harm, abuse, fraud, or security threats.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information, including data minimization, local-first storage where practical, permission controls, and review of third-party services. However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Rights

Depending on the privacy laws that apply where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, export of, or restrictions on the processing of your personal information. You may also have the right to withdraw consent or lodge a complaint. Please contact us at zengjianchi@outlook.com.

For content stored only on your device, you can usually remove it directly by deleting history items, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app.

If the app shows ads, you may also have choices about personalized advertising in regions where consent or opt-out controls are required, whether through in-app prompts, device settings, or tools offered by the ad provider.

10. Children's Privacy

Fast Image Text Scanner is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children as defined by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to address the issue.
