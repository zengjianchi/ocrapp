Before You Contact Us

The tips below solve most common issues with image import, OCR accuracy, and saved scan history.

Camera or Photos access

If the app cannot open the camera or photo library, check your device settings and make sure access has been granted for Fast Image Text Scanner.

OCR result quality

Text recognition works best with sharp images, strong lighting, high contrast, and text that is not heavily tilted, blurred, or blocked.

Saved scan history

Recent scans are stored locally on your device for quick access. If you delete the app or clear local data, saved history may no longer be available.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan text from a photo?

Open the app, choose the camera or import option, select or capture an image, and wait for the text extraction to finish. You can then review, edit, copy, or share the result.

Can I scan more than one image at a time?

Yes. The app supports batch import so you can convert multiple images in one flow and review the results together.

Why does the extracted text contain mistakes?

OCR accuracy can vary depending on image quality, handwriting, unusual fonts, glare, shadows, perspective distortion, or mixed languages in one image. Retaking the photo more clearly usually improves results.

Where is my data stored?

Scan history and related app data are generally stored locally on your device. For more details, please read our Privacy Policy.

Is the app free to use?

Yes. The current version of Fast Image Text Scanner is fully free to use for scanning, batch conversion, copying, and sharing results.
